Selected engineering work from my internship at Haiwu Technology, where I observed manufacturing operations and contributed to process, layout, assembly, and standardization work.

OrganizationHaiwu Technology

The environment

My internship placed me close to real manufacturing operations: components, heat-exchanger assemblies, production equipment, and the people responsible for turning engineering requirements into repeatable work.

This page is intentionally visual. As I refine the case study, I’ll add only details that accurately represent my own contribution and that are appropriate to share publicly.

Engineering in practice

From observation to physical detail.

The work involved understanding how parts were produced and assembled, documenting dimensions and process details, and thinking about how the workflow could be made more consistent and efficient.

Manufacturing systems

Beyond individual components, I was exposed to the broader production system: material flow, workstations, equipment, standardization, and the relationship between shop-floor processes and operational management.

The most valuable part of the experience was seeing how engineering decisions become real processes on a manufacturing floor.
